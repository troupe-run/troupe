An escalation is how an agent gets a decision it can't make itself: it does the research first, then brings a brief
to the right person. Escalation isn't built yet, and most of how it works isn't decided.

## What's decided

- **Research first.** Before an agent asks anyone, it runs the research steps your theatre defines, such as checking
  the issues, reading the docs and looking up the relevant standard. Then it brings a decision brief, not a bare
  question.
- **It goes to a role,** never to "the human". Whoever holds the role, a person or an agent, picks it up. See
  [Roles and actors](roles-and-actors.md).
- **An escalation that can't resolve itself raises a cue:** an open request to the role, with its brief, shown in
  notifications and the UI's inbox, as a [gate](gates-and-decisions.md)'s is. ("Cue" is a provisional name.)

## Not decided yet

How your theatre defines its research steps, what a decision brief contains, when an agent escalates rather than
decides, and how the brief and its answer are recorded: all of these are open, in
[#126](https://github.com/troupe-run/troupe/issues/126). Reaching a role is part of gates and escalations, in
[#62](https://github.com/troupe-run/troupe/issues/62).
