Notes are what a run teaches, fed into the next one, so your process gets better each time it runs.

Notes aren't built, and nothing about how they work is decided yet: what a note is and who or what writes one, where
notes are kept, how a later run uses them, and how you review or remove one. All of that is open, in
[#125](https://github.com/troupe-run/troupe/issues/125).
